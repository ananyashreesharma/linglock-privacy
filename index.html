LingLock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

The short version

LingLock doesn't track you, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your learning stays on your phone. The only time anything leaves your device is when you choose to send us a phrase report or a message, and then only what you typed or picked.

What stays on your device

Your language, your settings (such as whether we teach you the words a woman or a man would say, and how the script is shown), and your progress (phrases you've seen, marked "Got it", starred or pinned) are stored only on your device. They are shared only between the LingLock app and its widgets using Apple's on-device App Group storage, and are never sent to us.

What you can choose to send us

LingLock has two optional forms. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

  • Report a phrase (the flag on a phrase): the phrase you're reporting, the language, which version you were viewing (for example the woman's or man's wording), the problem you picked, your optional comment, the app version, and your email address only if you enter one so we can tell you when it's fixed. Without an email, reports are anonymous.
  • Talk to us (the 💬 button at the top of any screen): the topic you pick, your message, the language you're learning, the app version, and your email address only if you enter one so we can reply. The same form is on our support page, which sends your topic, message and optional email.

These are delivered to us by email through Formspree, a form-delivery service. We use them only to fix phrases, improve LingLock and reply to you. We never sell them or use them for advertising. If you're offline, the app keeps the message on your device and sends it the next time you open LingLock. You can ask us to delete anything you've sent.

Analytics, tracking, and third parties

  • No analytics SDKs
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers, and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • The only network use is sending a report or message you choose to send (via Formspree)

Text-to-speech

When you tap the speaker on a phrase or widget, LingLock uses Apple's on-device text-to-speech to read it aloud. This happens on your device and nothing is sent to us.

Children's privacy

LingLock is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us their email address, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions, or want something you sent us deleted? Use Talk to us in the app (the 💬 button at the top of any screen), or our support page.
