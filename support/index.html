Hi! LingLock is our own small app, and I'm here to help however I can. A language or phrase you'd love, something that isn't working, an idea, or just hello: write it below. I read every message myself.

What's it about?

Want to chat? Add your email and I'll write back personally. We can keep the conversation going there. I only use it to talk with you.

Quick answers

  • How do I add the widget? Lock your phone, long-press the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, tap the widget area under the clock, and choose LingLock.
  • A phrase sounds wrong? Tap the flag on that phrase in the app. You can add your email there too, and I'll let you know when it's fixed.
  • Your data: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
